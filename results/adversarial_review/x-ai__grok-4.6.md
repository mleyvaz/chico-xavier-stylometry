1. **[critical][veracity]** Section 4.3 table, Cruz e Sousa vs. genuine control: `"0.300 (15) | 0.200 (15) | 0.700 (15)"`. The JSON has **the same exact p = 0.2666… (4/15) on all three metrics**, with `n_permutations_used: 15`. 0.300 and 0.700 are not possible under exact enumeration of 15 assignments (k/15 ∈ {0, 0.067, 0.133, …, 1}); they are leftover or invented. This is a direct falsification of the claim that “Every number below is traceable to `results/statistical_summary.json`.”

2. **[critical][veracity]** Section 4.2: `"all remain nominally "significant""` for chunk-level tests. JSON contradicts this: Castro Alves cosine vs. genuine control **p = 0.199**, vs. baseline **p = 0.0654**; Cruz e Sousa cosine vs. genuine control **p = 0.1848**. The exploratory layer is misreported in the only place it is summarized.

3. **[critical][argument]** Title (`"Stylometric Evidence for Poly-Authorship"`) and the stated H1 (Intro: psychographed texts are `"measurably closer to those authors' independently known writing than to the medium's own baseline"`). The only FDR-surviving result is André Luiz vs. baseline — a persona **with no genuine control**, so it cannot instantiate H1 as defined. The only well-powered genuine-author test (Humberto vs. genuine, p = 0.002–0.004) goes the **other way**, and the actual H1 test (role-swap) is 6/9 directional, **zero** tests surviving FDR. The title asserts the hypothesis the results do not support.

4. **[critical][argument]** Abstract / §5: Humberto psychographed is `"statistically indistinguishable from Chico Xavier's baseline (the pattern H0 predicts)"` with vs.-baseline p = **0.057 / 0.314 / 0.514, n = 35 assignments**. Failure to reject at 35 exact permutations (minimum possible p = 1/35 ≈ 0.029) is not indistinguishability; Burrows is one permutation away from p < 0.05. This is the textbook “absence of evidence = evidence of H0” error, used to manufacture a clean H0 story for one persona.

5. **[critical][argument]** §4.5 vs §5 on André Luiz. §4.5: `"moved in one direction only … (0.030 → 0.007)"`. §5: `"moved in a single consistent direction … (p = 0.015 → 0.030 → 0.007)"`. Those two sentences cannot both be true: 0.015 → 0.030 is a move **toward** non-significance. The “we trust it because it was monotonic” warrant is false on the paper’s own numbers (which are not in the JSON in any case).

6. **[critical][argument]** Genre/register confound vs. the “load-bearing” André Luiz result. Baseline = spoken TV interview + personal letters + Q&A answers (123 chunks, 3 documents). André Luiz = 10 *Nosso Lar* novels (593 chunks). Cosine observed = 0.590 vs perm mean 0.563 (Δ = 0.027); Burrows 1.009 vs 0.914. A tiny shift between **literary afterlife-prose** and **letters/interviews** is then sold as `"measurably, robustly distinct from his own documented voice — consistent with, though not proof of, an independent stylistic source"`. No within-baseline register control (letters vs. interviews vs. André Luiz) is reported. §4.6 even places André Luiz **in the same UMAP region as the baseline**. Distinctiveness from a register-mismatched baseline is not evidence of poly-authorship.

7. **[major][argument]** Multiple-comparison shell game. Methods: FDR family = `"all 15 primary book-level p-values we report as evidence"` = 9 role-swaps + 6 Emmanuel/André Luiz vs.-baseline. Simultaneously, Humberto vs. genuine (p = 0.002–0.004, 3 metrics) is `"fully load-bearing"` / `"the study's one fully stable, well-powered finding"` — **outside** that family, unadjusted. Vs.-control and vs.-baseline for Castro Alves / Cruz e Sousa / Humberto (~18 extra tests) are also shown in Table 4.3 and used narratively, unadjusted. Tests are primary when they win, exploratory when they would inflate the family.

8. **[major][argument]** Triplicating correlated metrics inside BH is what lets André Luiz “survive.” All three André Luiz p-values are **identical** (2/286 = 0.006993), as expected if the same book permutation is extreme on redundant distances (`"correlated by construction"`, §3.2). BH with m = 15, ranks 1–3, yields p_adj = 0.006993 × 15/3 = **0.03497**. A single combined test would give 0.007 × 15 ≈ 0.105 (or, with m = 5 comparisons, depends on pooling). The “first result in five revisions to survive correction” is partly a multiple-testing **design choice**, not a discovery.

9. **[major][argument]** Discrete permutation floors presented as if they were continuous p < 0.05 evidence. Castro Alves: every book-level p = 0.100 because **min p = 1/10**. Role-swap cosine p = 0.050 is **1/20**, the most extreme assignment possible, not a measured tail probability. Cruz e Sousa role-swap uses **10** assignments; Humberto vs. baseline uses **35**. §4.4 then says two tests `"cross the conventional raw p<0.05 threshold"` including Castro Alves cosine **p = 0.050**, which does not cross p < 0.05. With these n’s a permutation test cannot support the claims being hung on it.

10. **[major][argument]** Unequal “books” as exchangeable units. Castro Alves psychographed: **2 chunks / 2 books** (table) but §6 `"2 short poems"`; Cruz e Sousa: 7 chunks / 4 books; baseline letters: **106 letters collapsed to 1 document** (85k words); André Luiz: 10 novels. Permuting these as equal units is not a “correct exchangeable unit” without length-weighting or a stated aggregation rule (JSON does not specify). Inflating anthology excerpts to “books” is what produces 286/495/56 assignment counts that look well-powered.

11. **[major][veracity]** Front matter: `"Every number below is traceable to results/statistical_summary.json (rounded to 3–4 significant figures)"`. Not true. Untraceable: 18/85/20 chunk split, 114 turns, 85,250 and 20,295 words, 41 documents (arithmetic on book counts, not a JSON field), UMAP geometry (§4.6), all previous-revision p-values (0.015, 0.030, 0.111, 66→286, 8/9→4/9→6/9), `"p = 0.002–0.004 … unaffected by any baseline change across five revisions"`. Rounding is also inconsistent: silhouette −0.01751 → `−0.018`; p = 0.04848 → `0.049` (toward significance); PERMANOVA F kept at 7 “significant” figures.

12. **[major][argument]** Global tests used as atmosphere. PERMANOVA F ≈ 493,000 / 663,000, p = 0.0, on **overlapping sliding windows** (JSON `n_chunks: 1874`). Silhouette **−0.018** (embedding) / **0.062** (stylometric) says there is **no** cluster structure. The paper correctly downgrades PERMANOVA, then still leads Results with it. Negative silhouette is the result that should constrain every later “independent stylistic profiles” claim; it does not.

13. **[major][writing/argument]** H1 is silently weakened whenever convenient. Defined as closer-to-genuine-than-to-baseline; operationalized for Emmanuel/André Luiz as `"distinctiveness from baseline"` (`"weaker sense"`, §2.1); then read as `"not simply Chico Xavier speaking in his ordinary register"`. That weaker hypothesis is what any genre shift would satisfy. A single-hypothesis framing is declared inadequate only after H1-as-stated failed.

**Verdict.** This draft is not ready to show a human co-author. It contains at least one table of p-values that does not match the JSON it cites as ground truth, a title that contradicts its own H1, an “indistinguishable = H0” claim the permutation floors cannot support, a “monotonic p-value” story that is internally non-monotonic, and a single FDR win that is genre-confounded, tiny in effect size, and partly an artifact of triplicating correlated metrics. Fix the numbers, drop or rewrite the title, stop treating n ∈ {10, 20, 35} exact tests as load-bearing, and run a register-matched negative control before this is a paper rather than a changelog of LLM-assisted corpus tweaks.
