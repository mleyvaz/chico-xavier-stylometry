1. **[critical] Speech-vs-writing confound on the “decisive” H1 test.** Abstract / §4.4 / §7: “for every one of the three authors with a genuine control corpus, the psychographed text is closer to that author’s own historical writing than to Chico Xavier’s baseline voice, on all three metrics (9/9 comparisons).” The baseline is *spoken* interview turns (*Pinga-Fogo*, 1971); genuine controls and psychographed books are *written* literary Portuguese. H1 as operationalized is therefore “written text attributed to author A is closer to other writing by A than to a TV interview.” That is the default stylometric expectation under almost any H0 that is not the straw man “psychographed prose is indistinguishable from conversational speech.” §2.2 and §6 mention a “small baseline” and genre mismatch; they never treat modality as a confound that can by itself produce 9/9 “genuine closer.” Until a written Xavier control exists, §4.4 cannot adjudicate H1 vs H0.

2. **[critical] No hypothesis test of the quantity that is supposed to decide H1.** §4.4 table and “All nine comparisons — three authors × three independent distance families — agree in direction.” `genuine_closer_than_baseline` in the JSON is a boolean on two point estimates (`d_psychographed_to_genuine` vs `d_psychographed_to_baseline`). There is no permutation test, no CI, no test of \(\Delta d\). Three of nine rows use \(n_{\text{psych}}=1\) or \(3\). Directional vote-counting of untested distances is not “the decisive test,” and it is not what “a permutation test with these p-values and these n’s can actually support.”

3. **[critical] Humberto cosine “win” is a 0.006 difference sold as H1.** §4.4: “Cosine … 0.640 … 0.646 … Yes (marginal).” JSON: \(0.63979668\) vs \(0.64600222\). Same section: psychographed vs genuine cosine is *significantly large* (observed \(0.640\) vs perm mean \(0.610\), \(p=0.0\)). So on the semantic metric the psychographed Campos texts are detectably *not* the genuine Campos distribution and sit almost as far from baseline as from genuine. Calling this a replication “on all three metrics” and “the strongest single data point” overstates what the numbers say.

4. **[critical] H0 is quietly redefined into something already false.** H0 in §1 is a single stylistic attractor around the medium. §4.3: Emmanuel/André Luiz “ruling out the strongest form of H0 (indistinguishability from the medium’s own conscious voice).” Literary spirit-books vs 18 chunks of televised speech will reject that “strongest form” for any competent writer, medium or not. Distinctiveness from interview speech is not poly-authorship. The design never tests the relevant H0 (one written Xavier style vs several).

5. **[critical] \(n=1\) and \(n=3\) are counted in the 9/9 headline.** Abstract and §4.4 fold Castro Alves (1 chunk) and Cruz e Sousa (3) in with Campos (\(n=112\)). JSON `n_psychographed_chunks` is 1 and 3; §2.2 itself says those samples “should be read as suggestive, not confirmatory.” A single 500–1000-word window has no stable Burrows/centroid estimate. Including them in “9/9 comparisons” and “nothing forced them to point the same way” (§5) is sample-size laundering.

6. **[major] PERMANOVA pseudo-\(F\) values are not credible as reported.** §4.1: “pseudo-F = 127723.2 … 199817.9 … 9 groups, 959 chunks.” JSON matches the digits (`127723.180…`, `199817.879…`, `p_value: 0.0`). Values five orders of magnitude above typical PERMANOVA \(F\) on stylometric distances, combined with sliding 500–1000-word windows from a handful of books, strongly suggest non-independence (chunk-level permutation of book-level structure), a misscaled distance, or a bug. \(p=0.0\) then only means “books differ,” which nobody doubted. The paper treats this as global support that “nine author/group labels are statistically distinguishable.”

7. **[major] Negative/weak clustering is spun as compatible with H1.** §4.1: “silhouette = −0.014 … 0.132 … consistent with genuinely overlapping surface signatures … a pattern compatible with H1 … not diagnostic of H0.” JSON: embedding silhouette \(-0.01441\), DB \(4.699\); stylometric silhouette \(0.132\), DB \(1.725\); both on **958** chunks, not 959. Negative silhouette means chunks are on average closer to other labels than to their own. That is evidence *against* distinct authorial clusters in embedding space. “Compatible with H1” is rhetoric.

8. **[major] Castro Alves vs baseline is mostly non-significant and omitted.** JSON `psychographed_vs_baseline` for Castro Alves: Burrows \(p=0.0508\), cosine \(p=0.2128\), stylometric \(p=0.0136\). §4.3 reports vs-baseline tests only for Emmanuel/André Luiz (both \(p<0.001\), \(n=380/334\) vs 18). The underpowered historical case that fails 2/3 baseline tests is dropped from the narrative that personas are “not simply indistinguishable restatements of his own baseline voice.”

9. **[major] Multiple comparisons and non-independence of metrics.** Intro/abstract: “three independent … feature families.” §6.6 admits they “are therefore correlated by construction.” No correction across 3 authors × 3 metrics × 2 contrasts (+ PERMANOVA + silhouettes). Treating 9/9 directional agreements as independent replications is invalid; Burrows, cosine, and the concatenated stylometric vector are three views of the same chunks.

10. **[major] UMAP described in the text contradicts H1 and is dismissed ad hoc.** §4.5: Emmanuel, André Luiz, and *psychographed* Campos “in one broad region that also contains the baseline points, while the genuine-control authors occupy a separate region.” Genuine Campos is prose (*O Brasil Anedótico*, *O Monstro…*), not poetry, so “register confound (poetry vs. conversational prose)” does not explain psychographed-Campos clustering with the interview and away from genuine Campos. “2D UMAP layouts distort global distance” is used only when the figure points the wrong way; the calibrated distances that “point the opposite way” include the 0.006 cosine gap in (3).

11. **[major] Chunking inflates \(n\) and makes every \(p\) anti-conservative.** §2.1: “sliding windows of 500–1000 words”; Campos 112 vs 72, Emmanuel 380, André Luiz 334, total 959. Permuting chunk labels (§3.3) ignores book/author as the real sampling unit. With 5000 permutations, \(p=0.0\) is guaranteed once several long books are sliced. The paper never reports number of *works*, only chunks, and never does a book-level or blocked permutation test.

12. **[major] 958 vs 959 discrepancy never reported.** JSON `n_chunks_total` / PERMANOVA `n_chunks`: 959; `clustering_quality.*.n_chunks`: **958**. §4.1 cites PERMANOVA on 959 and silhouettes without saying a chunk was dropped (likely NaN features). The draft’s reproducibility claim (“verbatim from `statistical_summary.json`”) fails on the clustering \(n\).

13. **[major] “First study” / empty literature.** §1: “to our knowledge, the first study to test H1 vs H0 for this corpus…” References end with a note that domain literature “should be added by Maikel.” You cannot claim priority and skip the literature search in the same draft. Prior authorship/stylometry discussion of Xavier exists; asserting a gap while parking citations on a co-author is not a limitation, it is an incomplete paper.

14. **[major] Abstract significance line is false as written.** “The best-powered case, Humberto de Campos … is individually significant on every metric (p<0.001 to p<0.001).” JSON Campos vs genuine: all `p_value: 0.0`; vs baseline cosine `0.0002`. The range “p<0.001 to p<0.001” is meaningless, and “every metric” does not specify which contrast. Combined with (3), this is promotional precision.

15. **[minor] Rounding that hides a fourth decimal.** §4.2 Cruz e Sousa Burrows \(p=0.004\) vs JSON `0.0044` (5000 perms → 22/5000). Three-decimal rounding toward stronger significance; 0.0766→0.077 and 0.1728→0.173 are fine, 0.0044→0.004 is the sloppy one. pseudo-\(F\) 127723.2 / 199817.9 and distances to 3 dp are faithful.

16. **[minor] Numbers not in the JSON, contrary to the header.** Header: “All figures cited below are reproduced verbatim from `results/statistical_summary.json`…” §2.1 “114 turns → 18 chunks,” “over 400 books” (abstract) vs “roughly 490” (§1), window 500–1000, 60 function words, 5000 permutations, 384-d MiniLM — none are in the supplied JSON. 18 baseline chunks *is* in the JSON; 114 turns is not. Internal 400 vs 490 is a veracity fail even without the JSON.

17. **[minor] Methods taxonomy does not match the three distances.** §3.1 lists classical stylometry, syntax, and dense semantics as three families; §3.2’s third distance is Euclidean on “the standardized classical/syntactic feature vector.” Burrows is a fourth representation. The mantra “lexical, syntactic, and dense semantic” (§1, §7) is a forced triad, not the analysis that was run.

18. **[minor] Citations: real but thin, one standard-ghost, APA uneven.** Burrows (2002), Reimers & Gurevych (2019), McInnes et al. (2018), Yule (1944) are real. Honnibal & Montani (2017) is the routinely cited *unpublished* spaCy 2 note, not a journal article; APA here invents a complete scholarly object. No Anderson (2001) for PERMANOVA, no Rousseeuw for silhouette, no citation for Davies–Bouldin. “EMNLP 2019” without pages/editors is incomplete APA.

19. **[minor] AI-academic prose tells.** Recurring template: three corpora, three families, three metrics, three authors; “end-to-end computational pipeline”; “pre-registered-style hypotheses” (not pre-registered); “convergent evidence”; “decisive test”; “best-powered case”; “to our knowledge, the first”; “we interpret this as evidence favoring H1 over H0” after listing fatal caveats; reproducibility theater in the header; Limitations as a neat numbered six. Hedging and overclaim alternate rather than constrain each other. The “Maikel before submission” aside belongs in a git comment, not a draft offered as scholarship.

**Verdict.** Do not show this to a human co-author as a results-and-claim draft. Digit-by-digit fidelity to most JSON fields is high, but the inferential leap (9/9 ⇒ H1 over H0) is unsupported: the baseline is speech, the decisive contrast is an untested pair of point distances, \(n=1/3\) rows are in the headline, Campos cosine is a tie, PERMANOVA \(F\sim10^5\) and chunk-level permutations are not a serious authorship test, and clustering/UMAP evidence is spun. Fix the design (written Xavier control, work-level or blocked tests, a real test of \(\Delta d\), drop or isolate \(n=1\)) or rewrite as a methods/corpus note with no H1/H0 winner. As it stands it is a well-typed overclaim on a modality confound.
