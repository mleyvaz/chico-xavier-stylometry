**1. Chico Xavier’s own (non-psychographed) prose**

- Short signed notes/prefaces (“Nota do médium”, “Explicação prévia”, “Palavras do médium”, etc.) appear in many FEB editions of his psychographed books (e.g. *Nosso Lar*, André Luiz, 1944; subsequent André Luiz and Emmanuel titles). These are the most readily available authentic samples of his own written voice.
- Personal letters have been published in scattered Spiritist compilations and biographies, but I am **UNCERTAIN** of any single, well-known dedicated collection with a stable title/publisher/year that can be cited as a corpus (search Brazilian digital libraries and archive.org for “cartas de Chico Xavier” or “correspondência de Francisco Cândido Xavier”).
- Print interviews and occasional signed pieces appeared in *O Reformador* (FEB) and other Spiritist periodicals, plus magazines such as *Manchete*. Dedicated interview-collection books exist in the secondary literature, but I am **UNCERTAIN** of precise titles that consist solely of his own words rather than mixed psychographed material.
