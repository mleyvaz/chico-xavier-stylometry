### 1. Chico Xavier's Non-Psychographed Prose (His Own Voice)

To capture Chico Xavier's baseline stylometric signature (written and spoken-transcribed), look for these specific publications:

    *   *Why it's useful:* This is the absolute best source for his personal written prose. It contains dozens of personal, non-psychographed letters written directly by Chico Xavier to Wantuil de Freitas (then president of the FEB) between the 1940s and 1970s.

*   **Pinga-Fogo com Chico Xavier** (Publisher: Edicel, 1972).
    *   *Why it's useful:* A direct print transcript of his famous 1971 live television interview. While originally spoken, it provides a massive corpus of his personal, non-dictated sentence structure and vocabulary.
*   **Chico Xavier - Mandato de Amor** (Publisher: União Espírita Mineira / UEM, 1992).
    *   *Why it's useful:* A biographical compilation that includes various personal notes, short speeches, and print interviews given by Chico.
