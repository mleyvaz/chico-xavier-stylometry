## 1. Chico Xavier’s non-psychographed / personally signed prose

- **“Explicação” / “Explicação necessária,” signed Francisco Cândido Xavier, in _Parnaso de Além-Túmulo_**.  
  First edition: Federação Espírita Brasileira (FEB), Rio de Janeiro, 1932. The introductory text is generally treated as Chico’s own account of the reception of the poems, rather than a text attributed to a named spirit. This is probably the most readily identifiable short primary text in his own prose.

- **Personally signed introductory notes in early FEB editions of Chico’s psychographed books** — useful targets include:
  - _Cartas de uma Morta_, attributed to Maria João de Deus; FEB, 1935.
  - _Emmanuel_, attributed to Emmanuel; FEB, 1938.
  - _Brasil, Coração do Mundo, Pátria do Evangelho_, attributed to Humberto de Campos; FEB, 1938.  
  These early editions commonly contain material identified as an explanation, note, or statement by the médium. **UNCERTAIN:** I cannot guarantee that every title above contains a separately titled Chico-signed note in every edition; this needs checking against scans of first/early editions.

- **_Chico Xavier: o homem e o médium_ — Elias Barbosa.**  
  Instituto de Difusão Espírita (IDE), Araras, first published 1973.  
  A book-length interview/biographical source containing Chico Xavier’s answers and reported statements. It is not his independently written prose, but it is a printed source for his own linguistic output and may include letters or reproduced statements. The interviews are generally presented as conversations with Chico, not spirit dictation.

- **_Chico Xavier: Pinga-Fogo_ — interviews conducted by Saulo Gomes.**  
  Various print editions, commonly published after the 1971 television broadcasts; editions have appeared from publishers including FEESP and others.  
  This is a printed transcript/source of Chico’s speech, but it derives from television interviews. Since you specifically exclude TV, it may be unsuitable unless your criterion is “published interview transcript” regardless of original medium.

- **_Testemunhos de Chico Xavier_ — Suely Caldas Schubert.**  
  Federação Espírita Brasileira (FEB). **UNCERTAIN:** This is a real Chico-related title, but I am not confident that it is a reliable source specifically for transcriptions of Chico’s authored letters rather than recollections/testimonies about him. Check contents before treating it as a primary corpus source.

- **Possible letter collections under the generic title _Cartas de Chico Xavier_.**  
  **UNCERTAIN:** Catalogues and second-hand listings do show publications described as Chico Xavier correspondence, but I cannot safely give a single editor/publisher/year without risking conflation with books of letters *to* Chico or books reproducing selected correspondence. ## 2. Psychographed poetry attributed to Castro Alves or Cruz e Sousa, beyond _Parnaso de Além-Túmulo_

- **_Castro Alves Fala à Terra_ — spirit-author Castro Alves; psychography of Francisco Cândido Xavier.**  
  Federação Espírita Brasileira (FEB), Rio de Janeiro, **1971**.  
  This is the clearest separate post-_Parnaso_ book attributed specifically to Castro Alves through Chico Xavier. It was associated with the centenary of Castro Alves’s death in 1871. It contains poetry and prose/messages attributed to Castro Alves.

- **_Antologia dos Imortais_ — psychography of Francisco Cândido Xavier.**  
  FEB; date varies by edition. **UNCERTAIN:** This is a real Chico Xavier poetry anthology of communications attributed to deceased literary figures, but I am not sufficiently confident, without consulting its table of contents, that it includes poems specifically signed by Castro Alves and/or Cruz e Sousa. It is nevertheless a high-priority title to inspect.

- **_Poetas Redivivos_ — psychography of Francisco Cândido Xavier; “Espíritos diversos.”**  
  FEB. **UNCERTAIN:** Real title, but I cannot confidently state that Castro Alves or Cruz e Sousa appears in it without checking the contents/index.
